GoalNexusBusiness coordination, not a chain of agents.

A five-stage method — delegate, assess, decide, execute, compound — expressed as a Carbon React runtime.

Get started
Build and preview

Delegate the outcome

Humans set the goal, deadline, and weighted trade-offs. Nothing in the runtime overrides those weights.

Assess the mode

Known path runs as a workflow. Unknown path switches to elicitation until policy, KPIs, and constraints are captured.

Decide with arbitration

Plans are scored against human KPI weights. Conflicts are suppressed, merged, or escalated — and logged.

Execute with control

The chosen plan runs while you watch, intervene, redirect, or override. Every intervention is a first-class event.
